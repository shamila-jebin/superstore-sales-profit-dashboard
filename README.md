# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profit Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Superstore Sales & Profit Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It analyzes sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, and regional performance to support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business metrics through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, and slicers.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze sales and profit trends over time.
- Compare sales performance across product categories and sub-categories.
- Evaluate regional sales and profitability.
- Understand sales distribution across customer segments.
- Identify the top 10 products by sales.
- Identify loss-making products.
- Create an interactive dashboard for business performance monitoring.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** — Measures and KPI calculations
- **Power Query** — Data preparation and transformation
- **Sample Superstore Dataset** — Sales data analysis

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Profit Margin %
- Average Order Value
- Average Discount
- Total Customers

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Sales Trend
Analyzes sales performance over time to identify growth patterns and fluctuations.

### 2. Profit Trend
Tracks changes in profitability over time and compares product categories.

### 3. Sales by Category
Compares sales across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.

### 4. Sales by Sub-Category
Ranks product sub-categories based on total sales.

### 5. Regional Performance
Explores sales performance across states and compares regional profitability.

### 6. Segment Analysis
Visualizes sales distribution across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

### 7. Top 10 Products
Displays the ten highest-selling products along with sales, profit, and quantity.

### 8. Loss-Making Products
Identifies products with negative profit for further business analysis.
